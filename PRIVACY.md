# Privacy Notice

The application is an educational simulation. It does not require an account and
does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment details.

Experiment configuration and result files are processed to validate and display
the data requested by the user. Imported files should not contain personal or
confidential information.

The donation page loads PayPal's payment software only when that page is opened.
PayPal processes payment and device information under its own privacy terms. The
application does not receive or store payment-card details.

Hosting infrastructure may retain ordinary request logs such as timestamps,
requested paths, response status, and network address for security and operational
purposes. Retention depends on the hosting provider's configuration.

If analytics, accounts, durable result storage, or additional third-party services
are introduced, this notice must be updated before those features are enabled for
the public.
